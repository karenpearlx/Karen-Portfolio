Systems Behind the Numbers

The scaffolding, not just the outcome.

Wins repeat when the system holds. Here's what I built alongside the campaign so the results didn't depend on me being awake.

01

Campaign Win Rate

Internal analytics tool that scores every content campaign on rank, clicks, and citation lift so we know what to kill and what to double down on.

02

Operator SOPs

Playbooks and training guides for blog QA, briefs, and publishing so new operators ramp in days, not weeks.

03

AEO/GEO Guides

Structural guidance for quick-answer capsules, comparison tables, and FAQ blocks tuned to how AI Overviews actually cite pages.

04

Publish Checklists

CMS-specific checklists for Webflow, Notion, and Framer so nothing ships with a broken schema, missing meta, or bad canonical.

05

Client Reporting

Lightweight GSC + win-rate reporting cadence that surfaces momentum, not vanity, to founders on a weekly rhythm.

06

AI Workflow Tooling

Cursor and Claude workflows to speed up briefs, audits, and outline generation without losing editorial control.

Method note

Days to win = first Campaign Win Rate check that flagged a blog win minus first publish date. Client names have been anonymized. Distribution counts reflect mapped wins by client (top nine + others).
