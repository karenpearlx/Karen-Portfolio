Systems behind the numbers

The setup, not just the result.

Wins repeat when the setup holds. Here's what I built alongside the campaign so the results didn't depend on me being awake.

01

Campaign win rate

A tool I built to score each content push on rank, clicks and citations. It told me what to kill and what to double down on.

02

Operator guides

Step-by-step guides for blog QA, briefs and publishing, so new hires got up to speed in days instead of weeks.

03

AEO/GEO playbook

Layouts for quick answers, comparison tables and FAQs, tuned to how AI Overviews actually pick pages to cite.

04

Publish checklists

Checklists for Webflow, Notion and Framer so nothing ships with broken schema, missing meta or a wrong canonical.

05

Client reports

Simple weekly reports mixing Search Console and win rate. Real progress, not vanity numbers.

06

AI workflows

Cursor and Claude flows that speed up briefs, audits and outlines without losing the editorial touch.
