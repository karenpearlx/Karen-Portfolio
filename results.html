Systems Behind the Numbers

The setup, not just the result.

Wins repeat when the setup holds. Here's what I built next to the campaign so results didn't rely on me being awake.

01

Campaign Win Rate

My own tool that scores each content push on rank, clicks, and citations. Tells me what to kill and what to double down on.

02

Operator Guides

Step-by-step guides for blog QA, briefs, and publishing so new hires get up to speed in days, not weeks.

03

AEO/GEO Playbook

Layouts for quick answers, compare tables, and FAQs tuned to how AI Overviews actually pick pages to cite.

04

Publish Checklists

Checklists for Webflow, Notion, and Framer so nothing ships with a broken schema, missing meta, or wrong canonical.

05

Client Reports

Simple weekly reports mixing GSC and win rate. Shows real progress, not vanity numbers.

06

AI Workflows

Cursor and Claude flows that speed up briefs, audits, and outlines without losing the editorial touch.

How it works

Days to win = the first check that flagged the post as a win, minus the day it was posted. Client names are hidden. Counts show mapped wins per client (top nine plus others).
