Content Strategy

Most blog content is published and forgotten. Mine is built to rank, planned around real search intent, written for humans, structured for Google, and tracked until it shows up where it's supposed to. No fluff, no filler, no posts that exist just to fill a calendar.

Schema

My process for ranking blogs.

01

Keyword research with intent in mind

Pick keywords by volume, difficulty, and what the searcher actually wants. Informational, commercial, transactional, each one needs a different shape of post. Skip the vanity terms that bring traffic but no buyers.

02

SERP analysis before the brief

Look at the top 10 for the target keyword. What format ranks, listicle, guide, comparison? What's the average word count? What questions does Google show in People Also Ask? The brief is built off what's already winning, then sharpened to beat it.

03

On-page that earns the click

Title tags and meta descriptions written to compete in the SERP, not generated by a plugin. Clean H1/H2 hierarchy, target keyword and variations placed where they belong, internal links to and from supporting posts, image alt text, schema markup where it fits.

04

Content built for the reader and the crawler

Clear intro, scannable subheads, short paragraphs, real examples, original insight. Long enough to fully answer the query, short enough to respect the reader's time. Google rewards the post people actually finish.

05

Track, refresh, repeat

Watch rankings and clicks in Search Console. Posts on page two get a refresh: better intro, stronger structure, new internal links, updated info. The biggest wins usually come from improving what's already close, not chasing new posts.
