About

What I actually do.

I started as a VA at an SEO agency and left a few years later running 30+ accounts. Audits, on-page, local, GBP, schema. The boring parts, not the strategy deck version.

Now operations at an AI citation platform. 14 clients, their whole content workflow, and the contractors behind it. 801 blogs published, and 812 keywords tracked daily to see which ones actually landed.

My first week there I built a React and Supabase dashboard because tracking lived in six spreadsheets nobody trusted. The team switched and never went back. More apps followed.

I'm not a developer by training. I'm just good at working out what needs to exist, then making it exist.
