How I work

A few things I'm stubborn about.

01

No temporary fixes

If something is duct tape I'll call it duct tape. I'd rather lose a day building it properly than spend a month unpicking it later.

02

Track it or it didn't happen

Every campaign I ran had a number attached to it. If I can't show you what moved, I assume nothing did.

03

I'll tell you what's broken

Including when it's your process, or something I set up myself. I'd rather have the awkward conversation early than explain a bad quarter later.

04

Learn it on the job

React, schema markup, AI search optimization. None of it was on my resume before a project needed it.

05

Good ops is boring

Nothing on fire, nobody chasing anybody for a status update. That's what I'm going for. Not heroics.

06

Write it down

Anything I did twice turned into a document. It's the only reason the work held up when I was offline.
